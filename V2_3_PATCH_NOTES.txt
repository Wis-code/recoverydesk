RECOVERYDESK V2.3 — JOB INTEGRITY & LEGAL RECORD HOTFIX

CORE RULE
Only these people can change an entered recovery job:
- Owner / Administrator / Sub-Administrator
- The Worker currently assigned to that specific job

Finance and every other Worker get read-only operational job records.

WORKER SUBMISSION LOCK
- New jobs begin as Draft.
- The assigned Worker can press "Submit job record".
- After submission, the Worker can no longer silently alter it.
- Every later Worker edit requires a written reason of at least 10 characters.
- RecoveryDesk stores the reason, worker identity, timestamp, and before/after values in jobEdits and Audit.
- This is enforced both in the UI and Realtime Database Security Rules.

LEGAL / COMMERCIAL DOCUMENTS
- Finance cannot generate agreements, invoices or receipts for a job.
- Other Workers cannot generate documents for someone else's job.
- Administrator/Sub-Administrator/Owner or the assigned Worker can generate documents.
- Every generated document is an immutable snapshot.
- Database rules allow document creation only; existing document records cannot be overwritten or deleted.
- Corrections therefore create a new document/version rather than rewriting the old legal record.
- Job pages clearly show Device Owner, Customer, Signer and Assigned Worker.

PAYMENTS
- Finance can still record/reconcile payments.
- An assigned Worker can record payments for their own job.
- Other Workers cannot alter payments for that job.

IMPORTANT
The warning inside the app says submitted-record edits are auditable and may have legal/compliance consequences if changed inaccurately. RecoveryDesk records accountability; it does not itself determine legal liability.

DEPLOYMENT
1. Publish database.rules.json first.
2. Then deploy all V2.3 files to GitHub main.
3. Wait for GitHub Pages green check.
4. Force refresh / reopen the PWA.
