RECOVERYDESK V2.3 FINAL

This is the final planned V2.3 feature release. Future changes should be driven by real user feedback, security issues, or operational needs.

FINAL ADDITIONS
- General Job Board for Admin/Sub-Admin/Owner to post work
- Work posts include deadline, priority, instructions and optional linked recovery job
- Active Workers can claim available work; first successful claim wins
- Claimed linked recovery jobs are assigned to the claiming Worker
- Admin can cancel board posts and unassign linked work when appropriate
- Worker Expenses & Money declarations
- Worker declarations cover personal expenses or customer money personally received
- Worker declarations are immutable after submission
- Finance/Admin review/reconcile worker declarations in a separate review record
- Full banking secrets are deliberately not collected; only account label and last 4 digits are supported
- Mandatory first-visit education for every top-level tab, including Owner/Admin
- Training is role-aware and cannot be skipped the first time
- Training completion follows the user's account across devices
- Settings includes Replay All Training
- Owner-only Test Data Cleanup
- New test customers can be explicitly marked as dummy/test records
- Cleanup detects marked or clearly named Test/Dummy/Sample records, shows counts, and requires DELETE TEST DATA confirmation
- Job/device/document/payment/task/worker-money test records are removed as a linked cleanup set

ALREADY INCLUDED IN V2.3
- Worker job ownership and server-side edit restrictions
- Submitted-job edit reasons with before/after audit history
- Owner/Admin/Sub-Admin job controls
- Finance operational read-only separation
- Immutable generated legal/commercial document snapshots
- Owner/customer/signer/assigned-worker clarity on job records
- Payment reconciliation
- Password reset and email/password change flow
- Email update / email invoice compose workflow without Firebase Storage billing
- Customer follow-up and milestone communication records
- Owner Administrator and Sub-Administrator hierarchy

STORAGE / AUTOMATIC EMAIL LIMITATIONS
Firebase Storage photo/signature/profile-image uploads remain dormant until billing is resolved.
The current email buttons open the user's configured email app with customer details/message prepared. True unattended automatic email delivery still needs a trusted mail backend/provider.
