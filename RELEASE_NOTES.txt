RECOVERYDESK V2 — RELEASE NOTES

Brand
- Uses the supplied WISCODE green geometric logo throughout the app and PWA icons.
- Light, Dark, and Follow-device modes.
- Intentional SVG iconography and semantic status colors.
- Green shades represent forward progress; amber/red signal attention or blocked work.

Identity & access
- Existing Firebase Admin account remains compatible.
- Email/password and Google sign-in.
- New Staff can create a sign-in and request access.
- Admin approves Staff and assigns Admin / Worker / Finance role and job title.
- Staff may change their display name; real name remains underneath and Admin-controlled.
- Staff/Admin can approve customer portal access.
- Customers cannot edit their registered customer name.

Customers, jobs & devices
- Permanent Customer IDs: CUS-YYYY-####.
- Permanent Device IDs: DEV-YYYY-####.
- Job IDs: DR-YYYY-####.
- Returning customers and returning devices keep their history.
- One job can contain multiple devices.
- Separate device owner, submitter, relationship, signer, and signer authority.
- Legacy V1 single-device jobs can be migrated from Settings without deleting the original job ID.

Intake controls
- Worker confirms assessment payment and physical authorization/signature.
- Standard payment methods include Moniepoint Transfer, POS, Cash, Bank Transfer, Other.
- Job remains Intake Pending until required checkpoints are satisfied.
- Intake photos can be made required later, after Storage is configured.

Recovery workflow
- Intake Pending
- Ready for Assessment
- Assessment
- Awaiting Approval
- Recovery In Progress
- Ready for Collection
- Completed / Closed / Blocked
- Customer portal can approve a recovery quote.
- Staff can confirm local/offline quote approval on behalf of a customer interaction.

Tasks
- Per-worker tasks with due date and priority.
- Overdue / Due Today / Upcoming / Completed sections.
- Job-linked tasks.
- In-app reminders only in V2; automatic background push notifications are intentionally deferred.

Finance
- Assessment fees, recovery payments, deposits, refunds, discounts, expenses, balances, and outstanding amounts.
- Daily, monthly, and all-time operational summaries.
- Worker can record/confirm job payments.
- Finance/Admin gets company-wide operational finance view.
- RecoveryDesk is an operational finance tracker, not a replacement for formal accounting software.

Documents
- Branded Invoice numbers: INV-YYYY-####.
- Branded Receipt numbers: RCT-YYYY-####.
- Branded Agreement numbers: AGR-YYYY-####.
- Print / Save as PDF from the browser.
- WISCODE INNOVATIONS LTD and RC 9656932 included.
- Agreement records device owner, submitter, signer, devices, fees, risk acknowledgement, confidentiality, and signatures.

Files & evidence
- UI supports device intake/condition photos.
- UI supports image/PDF upload of exact signed paperwork.
- Signed copies can be marked client-visible.
- Images larger than ~900 KB are compressed in the browser, with an iOS/Safari-compatible fallback.
- Recovered customer files/data are NOT intended to be uploaded to RecoveryDesk.
- File upload activates only after Blaze + default Firestore + Firebase Storage are configured and the included rules are published.

Audit
- V2 records key actions such as customer creation, device registration, job creation/migration, payment confirmation, staff approval, document generation, uploads, task completion, and settings changes.
